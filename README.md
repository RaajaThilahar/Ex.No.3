## **2. Food Menu Nutritional Chart**

### **Introduction**

A Food Menu Nutritional Chart is a structured representation of the nutritional profile of various dishes. It typically includes details such as *dish name, major ingredients, calorie count, protein value, fat content,* and *allergen alerts*. Presenting this in a *neatly organized table* helps diners quickly compare meals according to health goals, dietary restrictions, or calorie limits.
In modern settings such as restaurants, cafeterias, sports centers, hostels, and hospitals, these charts play a key role in guiding people toward healthier eating habits. With the application of AI-driven prompt techniques, such nutritional charts become easier to generate, customize, and personalize based on user needs. Below is the theoretical explanation of how different prompting strategies contribute to building effective nutritional charts.

---

## **Direct Instruction Prompt**

This technique gives the system a clear command to produce the chart.
**Example:**
“Generate a Food Menu Nutritional Chart containing dish name, primary ingredients, calories, protein, fat, and allergen information in a clean tabular format with a small food icon for each dish.”

**Application:** Ensures a properly structured, uniform table.
**Benefit:** Provides quick and consistent nutritional data.

---

## **Contextual Prompting**

This method modifies the output based on a user’s request.
**Example:**
“If the user asks for low-calorie meals, display only dishes under 400 calories with their nutritional information.”

**Application:** Produces targeted tables based on user goals.
**Benefit:** Reduces irrelevant items and improves personalization.

---

## **Persona-Based Prompting**

The system responds as a diet assistant or food expert.
**Example:**
“Act like a nutrition consultant and describe each dish’s nutritional value in a friendly, simple tone.”

**Application:** Adds a human-like explanation to support healthy decisions.
**Benefit:** Makes nutritional information easier to interpret.

---

## **Few-Shot Prompting**

The system learns from provided examples before generating the full table.
**Example:**
“Here are sample entries for two dishes. Follow the same pattern to create the remaining rows.”

**Application:** Ensures consistency in formatting.
**Benefit:** Reduces uneven or mismatched table styles.

---

## **Chain-of-Thought Prompting**

This technique builds the chart step by step.
**Example:**
“To prepare the Nutritional Chart:

1. List dish names
2. Identify important ingredients
3. Add calorie values
4. Include protein and fat content
5. Specify allergen warnings
6. Present everything in a comparison table
7. Add a food icon for each dish”

**Application:** Maintains order and thoroughness.
**Benefit:** Minimizes missing information.

---

## **Instruction With Constraints Prompt**

This method enforces rules for how the chart should appear.
**Example:**
“Use short ingredient descriptions, limit the table to seven rows, and include only one emoji per dish.”

**Application:** Helps maintain clarity and readability.
**Benefit:** Prevents clutter and overloading.

---

## **Reflective Prompting**

The system restates the user’s needs before showing the output.
**Example:**
“You want the nutritional details for this dish, correct? Here is the complete nutritional entry.”

**Application:** Ensures accuracy in understanding.
**Benefit:** Avoids mismatch between user intent and output.
